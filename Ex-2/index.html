🩸 What is LCU?

The Lokesh Cinematic Universe (LCU) is a shared universe of gritty Tamil films directed by Lokesh Kanagaraj. Featuring action, crime, and emotional storytelling — it connects powerful protagonists across movies like Kaithi, Vikram, and Leo.

  • 🚓 Undercover cops & drug mafias
  • 🎭 Emotional anti-heroes
  • 🔗 Interconnected plots & characters

🎬 Movies in the LCU

Kaithi (2019)

👤 Dilli (Karthi)
⚔️ An ex-convict helps police fight a drug mafia overnight.

Vikram (2022)

👤 Agent Vikram (Kamal Haasan)
🔍 A black-ops team takes on a powerful drug syndicate.

Leo (2023)

👤 Parthiban / Leo Das (Vijay)
💣 A café owner’s dark past as a gangster resurfaces.

🚀 Future of LCU

The LCU is expanding with character-based spin-offs like Rolex, sequels like Leo 2, and possible crossovers featuring Vikram, Dilli, and Leo in one epic saga!
